VidCompressor Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026 · Support

VidCompressor is a video utility app for compressing and trimming videos on your iPhone, iPad and Mac. This policy explains what information the app accesses and how it is handled.

Information We Collect

VidCompressor does not collect personal information, create user accounts, track users, or include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Photos and Videos

The app asks for access to your photo library only so you can choose videos to compress or trim, and so exported videos can be saved back to your library when you request it. On Mac, the app can also open and save video files that you choose.

Videos can contain metadata such as the recording date, location and camera details. You decide whether exported videos keep this metadata with the Keep Metadata option.

Videos you select are processed locally on your device. VidCompressor does not upload your videos to a server.

Generated Files

Exported videos may be temporarily stored on your device while you preview, save, or share them. These files are used only to provide the app's compression, trimming, saving, and sharing features.

Data Sharing

VidCompressor does not sell, share, or transfer personal data to third parties. When you use the system share sheet, you choose where to send the exported video.

Children's Privacy

VidCompressor is not designed to collect information from children, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when app features change. The latest version will be available at this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open a support request on GitHub or see the support page.
